BeInspo website: how to open it

1. Extract (unzip) this whole folder first. Opening a page from inside the zip
   without extracting breaks the styles and links.
2. In the extracted folder, double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
3. Every menu item, button and link now works between the pages.

  readiness-assessment.html      01 Readiness Assessment

  school-boards.html             Solved for school boards

  contact.html                   Contact form (sends only once the site is live)
  privacy.html, accessibility.html
