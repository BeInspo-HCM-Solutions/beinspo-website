Implementation Assurance

Going with another partner? We complete your team.

Your people know the organization better than anyone. What most teams haven’t had to do before is turn that into what to ask a partner for, and then judge whether what comes back is right. That is the gap Assurance fills: we join your team, not the partner’s, for the length of the program.

Who it is for, and who it is not for.

For

  • An organization that has selected, or is about to select, a large implementer and has a small internal team.
  • An organization whose partner contract covers configuration but leaves data, process and testing on the client side.
  • An organization mid-program that is seeing requirements drift and wants an independent view before testing starts.

Not for

  • An organization that wants us to build. That is Implementation.
  • An organization that wants a second implementer to compete with the first. We don’t compete with your partner; we complete your team.

What we need from you

A seat at the table, and your experts’ time.

  • Your subject-matter experts for a preparation session before each of your partner’s design sessions.
  • A seat for us in design sessions and access to the partner’s configuration environment for reviews.
  • An agreement with your partner that review findings are logged and answered; most partners welcome it, because it reduces rework for them too.

What it costs

Known for the length of the program, and independent by design.

A schedule of touchpoints agreed at the start, tied to your partner’s plan, so the cost is known for the length of the program. We don’t build and we don’t bill for build, which is what keeps the review independent.

Questions we are asked

Straight answers, before you ask.

Can you be independent when you also sell implementation?

In Assurance we don’t build, we don’t bill for build, and our findings go to you in writing. If a finding would be served by replacing your partner, we will say so, and we will not pitch for the work.

Will our implementer resent this?

Experienced partners prefer a client who asks for the right thing and reviews on a schedule; it reduces their rework. We set the working arrangement with them in the first week.

We’re already halfway through. Is it too late?

No. The highest-value moments, configuration review before test and the data migration, are usually still ahead.

Does Assurance replace our project manager?

No. Your PM runs the program; we supply the judgment on what to ask for and whether what came back is right.

Can you take on the data migration entirely?

Yes. It is the one part of Assurance where we do the work rather than review it, because it is where overruns come from.
