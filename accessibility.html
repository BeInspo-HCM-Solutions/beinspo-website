Accessibility

Accessibility statement.

We want everyone who works with a public-sector organization, or who is thinking about it, to be able to use this site and to work with us without barriers. This page says what standard we build to, what we have done, what we know still needs work, and how to tell us when something is in your way.

Last reviewed [date].

The standard we build to

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 at Level AA, the standard set out in Ontario’s Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation under the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA). We test against it before launch and after every significant change.

What we have done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a “skip to content” link.
  • Headings are structured in order so that screen readers can navigate by section.
  • Text and background colours meet the AA contrast ratio. Yellow is used for accents and large headings only, never for body text on white.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200 per cent without loss of content or function, and the layout works at phone width.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative images are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Diagrams, including the path, the five phases and the five support levels, are also presented as text or tables on the same page.
  • Every form field has a visible label; errors are described in text and announced to assistive technology; no field relies on colour alone.
  • The contact form has no CAPTCHA. We protect it in ways that do not ask you to prove you are human.
  • Links say where they go. We do not use “click here”.
  • The homepage message rotation pauses while you hover or focus on it, and does not run for visitors who have asked their device for reduced motion.

Documents

PDF documents offered on this site are produced with tags, reading order and alternative text. If you receive one that is not accessible to you, ask us for the same content in an accessible format and we will provide it within [5 business days] at no cost.
