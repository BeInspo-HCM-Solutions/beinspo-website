Privacy

Privacy statement.

BeInspo collects as little personal information as a website can. This page says what we collect, why, where it goes, how long we keep it, and how to ask us about it.

Effective [date]. Last reviewed [date].

Who we are

[Be Inspo HCM Solutions Inc.], [street address], Toronto, Ontario, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Questions about privacy go to [privacy contact name, title] at connect@beinspo.ca or +1 (437) 227-5399.

What we collect

Through the contact form, only what you type: your name, work email, phone number if you give it, your organization if you give it, where your organization is on the path if you choose an option, your message, and any file you attach.

Automatically, when you visit: the standard server log that every website keeps: your IP address, the pages you visited, the time, and the browser you used. [If an analytics tool is used, name it here, state that it is configured without cookies or with IP anonymization, and say what it records. If none is used, state: “We do not use analytics tools or advertising trackers.”]

We do not set marketing cookies, run advertising pixels, buy contact lists, or collect information from social networks.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare for a conversation you asked for.
  • To keep the website running securely and to diagnose faults, which is what the server log is for.
  • For nothing else. We do not add contact-form senders to a mailing list unless you ask to be added.

Where it goes

  • A contact-form submission is emailed to connect@beinspo.ca, a mailbox hosted by [Microsoft 365 / provider] in [Canada / the provider’s region]. The attachment travels with the email and is not stored on the website.
  • The form is delivered by [FormSubmit or the service chosen at launch], which processes the submission on our behalf and keeps [no copy / a delivery log for N days].
  • The website is hosted by [hosting provider] in [region]. Server logs stay with the host.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone else. We would disclose it only where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries, including attachments, are kept for [24 months] after our last contact with you, then deleted, unless the enquiry led to an engagement, in which case it becomes part of that engagement’s records.
  • Server logs are kept for [90 days].

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to connect@beinspo.ca. We reply within [30 days].
  • You can withdraw consent at any time; we will stop using your information for the purpose you withdrew it for, subject to legal or contractual limits, which we will explain.
  • If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Cookies

This site uses [no cookies / only the technically necessary cookies needed to protect the contact form from abuse]. No cookie is used to track you across sites.

Information we handle for clients

During an engagement we handle employee and financial data that belongs to a school board or other public-sector body. That data is not collected through this website and is not covered by this statement. It is handled under a written data-handling agreement with the client, aligned to the Municipal Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (MFIPPA) and the client’s own policies, and it is never used for any other purpose.

Changes

If this statement changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. We do not reduce the protection of information already collected without telling the people it belongs to.
