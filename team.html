How the firm was formed

From the inside.

We spent years on the board and college side of HCM and ERP programs: writing the requirements, sitting in the design sessions, running the payroll reconciliations, closing the year ends. We saw the same problems underestimated on program after program, because the people estimating them had never run a board. BeInspo exists to bring those problems already solved, so a program spends its time on what is unique to the organization in front of us.
