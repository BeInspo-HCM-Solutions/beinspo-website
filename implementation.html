Who it is for, and who it is not for.

For

Not for

Integrations & solution architecture

Your Oracle system doesn’t live alone. Neither does our work.

A public-sector Oracle implementation is never just Oracle. Scheduling, dispatch, time and learning systems all exchange data with the core, and someone has to own the end-to-end design. On our programs, that is us, and it is in scope from the first phase, not bolted on in the last.

Feasibility and vendor engagement

We sit in the room with your third-party vendors, assess what each integration can and cannot do, and surface the design decisions early: who owns costing rules, what runs nightly and what runs in real time, and what happens when a record fails.

End-to-end solution architecture

Data-flow design across system boundaries, error-handling and reconciliation patterns, and ownership models for shared reference data. Minimum viable, but genuine.

Build, test and cutover

Through to a true end-to-end test with real vendor files, not middleware mocks, and an agreed error-ownership model between teams before day one.

Typical integrations on a public-sector program

01 / 06 Both ways

Scheduling and dispatch systems

What flows

Demographics, assignments and eligibility out; final hours and cost codes back to time and payroll.

Owner question we settle early

Who owns costing rules: Oracle, middleware or the vendor.

02 / 06 Both ways

Time and scheduling (for example UKG)

What flows

Employees, assignments and absence balances out; approved time and premiums back in.

Owner question we settle early

Which system is the record for absence balances.

03 / 06 Out, with returns

Learning and certification

What flows

Employee and position data out; completed qualifications back to the person record.

Owner question we settle early

Whether qualifications gate assignment eligibility.

04 / 06 Out

Banking and payments

What flows

Direct deposit and payment files; returns and rejections reconciled.

Owner question we settle early

Who acts on a rejected payment and by when.

05 / 06 Out

Pension and benefits carriers

What flows

Enrolment, contribution and remittance files on the carrier’s schedule.

Owner question we settle early

How retroactive changes reach the carrier.

06 / 06 Out

Statutory and regulatory reporting

What flows

Year-end and periodic reporting, including T4 and Record of Employment.

Owner question we settle early

Who signs off each file before it leaves.

Questions we are asked

Straight answers, before you ask.

Can a firm your size staff an eighteen-month program?

The team is named before we start and committed for the program’s length. We do not run a pyramid, so there are no juniors to rotate off.

What if a key business resource leaves?

The living spec and the requirement dashboard keep the knowledge out of any one person’s head. Whoever steps in from your team picks up from the record, so the handover is easy and nothing is lost with the person who left.

Who owns the integrations end to end?

We own the build end to end: design, build, testing and cutover. The data is owned by your business and your vendors.

How many payroll reconciliation runs?

Two full payroll reconciliations before go-live, matched against your legacy system, plus full support through two go-live cycles. For teams that want to be extra cautious, we also offer a payroll parallel run before go-live.

[State Oracle partnership status when confirmed.]

Can we start with HCM and add ERP later?

Yes. The method is per pillar; a single-pillar program is shorter and the ERP pillar can follow.
