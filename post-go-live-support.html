Post Go-Live Support & Stabilization

Gone live and need it to settle?

The first pay cycle after go-live is rarely the hard one. The second is where retro corrections and back-dated changes surface, and year end is where everything is tested at once. We stay through both, with the same people who built the system, and your team runs the day-to-day on a fixed subscription.

Where are you today?

This is the Sustain phase continuing, with the same people who built the system.

Who you work with
The people who built the system, or, for an organization we did not implement, a named lead after a four-week stabilization assessment.

A subscription covers levels 1 to 3, run by your team; credits are consumed only when you assign work to us at levels 4 and 5.

A support model your team runs, a knowledge base built from what we have solved at public-sector organizations, and year ends that close on time.

Who it is for, and who it is not for.

For

  • An organization going live with us, where this is the Sustain phase continuing.
  • An organization already live on Oracle HCM or ERP whose implementer has rolled off and whose support model never fully formed.
  • An organization approaching its first year end on a new system.

Not for

  • An organization that needs a rebuild rather than stabilization. We will say so after the assessment, and Implementation Assurance or a scoped re-implementation is the honest answer.

What we need from you

One analyst, and the authority to call us.

  • A business systems analyst to hold the L3 role, trained by us if we implemented, or in the first four weeks if we did not.
  • Someone with authority to assign work to us at L4 and to set the application’s limits.
  • For an organization we did not implement, access to configuration and recent pay data for the stabilization assessment.

What it costs

A fixed cost until we’re called in.

A fixed subscription covers the application and levels 1 to 3, so your people handle the day-to-day without paying for ours. Credits are consumed only when work is assigned to us at L4 or L5, and you decide when that happens. There is no hourly meter running in the background.
